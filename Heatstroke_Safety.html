Prevention Measures
Build heat tolerance early 暑熱順化
Before the real heat hits, get into a habit of sweating on purpose — regular baths and walks condition your body to handle high temperatures.
Avoid heat, humidity & direct sun 高温・多湿・直射日光を避ける
Outdoors: use cooling gear, stick to shaded paths, and take breaks often. Indoors: set the AC to match room conditions and block direct sun with curtains or blinds.
Hydrate on a schedule, not on thirst 計画的・こまめな水分補給
Drink before you feel thirsty — thirst lags behind dehydration, especially for elderly people. Hydrate before bed, after waking, and around bathing. Replace salt too after heavy sweating.
Plan rest breaks for exercise & events 運動時等の計画的休憩
Athletes, organizers, and instructors should all know the signs of heat stroke. Restock with sports drinks after heavy sweating — even spectators cheering in the sun need to stay hydrated.
Never leave a child alone in a car 車内にこどもだけにしない
Car interiors heat up dangerously fast, even for what feels like "just a minute." Never leave a child alone inside a vehicle — not even briefly.
Symptoms & First Aid
Know the severity
Mild
Dizziness on standing, muscle cramps, heavy sweating.
立ちくらみ・筋肉痛（こむら返り）・大量の発汗
Moderate
Headache, nausea or vomiting, fatigue, trouble concentrating.
頭痛・吐き気/嘔吐・倦怠感・集中力低下
Severe
Confusion or impaired consciousness, convulsions, can't walk straight, very high body temperature.
意識障害・けいれん・歩行障害・高体温
First aid, step by step
1
Check if they respond normally
Call their name and ask a simple question. Is the response normal, confused, or absent?
If response is confused or absent → call an ambulance immediately. While waiting: move them to shade/AC, loosen or remove excess clothing, and cool the neck, armpits, and groin with ice or cold wet cloths. Do not try to make them drink — they could choke.
2
If they respond normally: cool them down
Move to a cool, shaded, or air-conditioned spot. Loosen clothing and cool the skin — fan them, use cold water, or apply ice packs to the neck, armpits, and groin.
3
Rehydrate with salt
If they can drink unaided, give water with a pinch of salt, an oral rehydration solution, or a sports drink.
4
Reassess — don't leave them alone
Improving and drinking fine on their own → keep resting in the cool spot with someone watching them. Not improving, or can't drink unaided → call an ambulance now.
119
When in doubt, call. Heat stroke can turn fatal quickly — treat it as an emergency, not something to wait out.
